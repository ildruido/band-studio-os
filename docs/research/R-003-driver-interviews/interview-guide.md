# R-003 interview guide and codebook

Part of the [R-003 protocol](protocol.md). Draft; translated into Italian before the first call. The interviewer does not pitch, does not react to answers, and asks about real past events.

## Opening (2 min)

Purpose: "We are researching how bands run the promotion for their gigs. There are no right answers and I'm not selling anything." Ask consent to take notes and record. Remind them they can skip any question or stop at any time.

## Part 1: the band today (3 min)

1. Tell me about your band: who is in it, how long you've played, how often you play live.
2. When was your last gig, and where?

## Part 2: unprompted problems (5 min). Ask before promotion comes up.

3. What are the biggest problems your band has right now? Let them list as many as they like.
4. Of everything you've mentioned, which five matter most? Put them in order.
5. Ask "anything else?" once. Do not suggest topics.

## Part 3: the last gig, step by step (12 min)

6. Walk me through how people found out about your last gig. Start from when it was booked.
7. Who made the flyer, poster or post? Who posted it, and where? Can you show it or tell me what it looked like?
8. How long did it take, in total, across everyone? Which part took the longest?
9. What did you do on the day of the gig, and after it?
10. Was anyone outside the band involved (a friend, a manager, the venue, an agency)? What did they do?
11. What do you not bother promoting, and why?

## Part 4: tools and shortcomings (5 min)

12. What do you use to make and post things? Why those?
13. What annoys you about that way of working? What goes wrong?
14. How does your band agree on a flyer or post? What happens when you disagree?
15. Have you tried something and given it up? What and why?

## Part 5: reach (3 min)

16. Where do bands like yours hang out, online or offline? Where would you hear about something that was for bands?
17. How many other active bands near you do you know? Name a few you'd say are like you.

## Part 6: prompted ranking (3 min, last)

18. Show a card with these 10 problems in random order and ask for the top three: finding gigs, rehearsal space, money, getting people to come to gigs, making flyers and posts, recording, finding bandmates, getting press, keeping the band together, time. Do not discuss promotion before this.

## Close (2 min)

Ask if there's anything they expected us to ask. Thank them. Mention how data is kept and their right to withdraw.

## Codebook

- **Promotion code (Q3 to Q5):** an item counts if it is about flyers, posters, social posts, announcing gigs, filling the room, press or "getting people to come". Not counted: finding gigs, booking.
- **Top five (Q4):** the participant's own ordered list. If they name fewer than five, the list stands as it is.
- **Promotion by member (Q6 to Q10):** yes if a band member made or posted the main promotion; no if someone outside did it all; mixed is coded yes if a member made at least the main asset. Note the evidence: shown, described or vague.
- **Shortcomings (Q13 to Q15):** time, quality, consistency, cost, approval delays, skill, other. Each mention coded once per interview.
- **Reach (Q16 to Q17):** channels named, count of other bands named.
- **Verbatim quotes:** copy the exact words and the participant code, no names.
