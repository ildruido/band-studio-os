# R-005 Gemini Deep Research prompt (route 2)

Written to be neutral: it names no product of ours and hints at no expected answer. Ale pastes it into Gemini Deep Research and returns the exported text (share links cannot be read). Its claims count only through sources opened by the lead.

```text
Research question: how do independent bands (unsigned or self-managed rock, indie and similar) actually promote their own gigs today, which tools and habits do they rely on, and what makes them stay with or change those tools?

Context: the person doing the promotion is usually one member of a small band with a day job and little money. Focus on bands in Italy and the UK first, then the rest of Europe, using English and Italian sources from 2025 onward.

Please report, with evidence:
1. What bands say they use to promote gigs (design tools, AI tools, social schedulers, link pages, venue or ticketing platforms, paid help from friends or designers, printed posters), and which they consider good enough, too slow, too expensive or too generic. Give attention both to people who are satisfied with free or cheap general tools and to people who are frustrated or who gave up.
2. Evidence on whether bands pay for promotion tools or services, how much, and what made them stop paying or never start.
3. How much time and effort bands say gig promotion takes them, and who in the band does it.
4. Any tools or services made specifically for bands or gigs (not general marketing tools) launched or updated in 2025 or 2026 that help create promotion material or run a gig promotion campaign, including ones that shut down or were abandoned, and why.
5. How venues, promoters and audiences react to AI-generated or template-based posters and captions, with country and date.

Sources and format: prefer first-hand voices (forum and community threads, interviews, band and venue posts, surveys with a stated sample), then independent journalism and reviews; treat vendor pages as vendor claims. For every claim give the source URL, publisher, date, country and a short quote, and say whether it is a first-hand voice, a survey, a journalist, or a vendor. Say what you could not find or open. Do not assume any particular answer.
```
