## Strongest evidence against our hypothesis (read first)

1. **Simplified now describes our flow, for general small businesses.** Its page says: "Brief Riley once. Riley creates your social posts, emails, and campaign assets, keeps everything in your brand, and gets it ready to publish. You review." (E-07). Its pricing page says "Nothing ships until you approve it", lists scheduling, and shows Pro at "$19 per month" billed annually (E-06). That price is not the cost of the whole job: the same page says AI features "are billed as you use them" and plan credits do not roll over, and no credit allowance per campaign is visible. A 79 USD "campaign-based Starter" plan was seen by Brand Tool Guide in October 2026 (E-18) but is not on Simplified's own page today (E-06); with 5 campaigns a month it would be about 16 USD a campaign, near our 15 EUR line. No flyer or story is shown for it. It is the closest thing to a G1 kill candidate, and it has no independent test. This is vendor material only (D), unconfirmed by any independent source, and the page does not show a flyer or a 9:16 story, so condition (a) is not shown. It is the closest match to our guided, approve-before-publish flow found so far.
2. **Canva's own AI is moving to the same flow.** Creative Bloq (C, 2026-04-16): "You can instantly generate content in multiple formats, making it a potential time-saver for multi-channel campaigns" and Canva has "a Memory Library. It can remember styles, helping to keep projects on brand" (E-01). Preview only: "currently available as a research preview".
3. **The cost line is not a barrier.** Canva Pro is 18 USD a month or 144 USD a year (E-03, E-04); PosterMyWall lists 13 USD a month (E-09); Mew Design's Hobby plan shows 5.69 USD a month, billing period not visible (E-08). The sticker prices sit under our 25 EUR line; the cost of a full gig set on credit-metered plans is unknown, so treat this as indicative only.

## Gemini route

Ale ran [gemini-prompt.md](gemini-prompt.md) in Gemini Deep Research and returned a 14-page report ("Promoting Independent Band Gigs With Current Design and Social Tools", 46 cited sources). The report itself is an AI summary (grade D); only claims whose cited page I opened count. **What Gemini concluded:** a non-designer can produce flyer, story, post, captions and scheduled posts inside one app for roughly £10 to £13 a month (Canva, Adobe Express, PosterMyWall, VistaCreate), but "no low-budget tool currently generates all five deliverables from a single gig brief in one hands-off flow". **Where it agrees with the desk route:** no tool is shown to do the whole job in one guided flow (G1 stays Insufficient evidence, Low, with both routes pointing the same way); Simplified and Canva AI are the nearest threats; Canva, PosterMyWall, Adobe Express and VistaCreate fit the price line at sticker price, while Simplified does not once its 29 USD monthly price and 19 USD bulk-scheduling add-on are counted (about 48 USD a month, E-17); time is unmeasured.
