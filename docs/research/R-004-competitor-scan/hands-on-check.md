# R-004 hands-on check: Canva (and PosterMyWall)

A person with a browser needs to do this once; the cloud environment cannot open these sites. About 1 hour. It settles whether a band can already get a gig campaign from them (S2 in [R-004](report.md), and the first criterion in R-005).

**Set up first (so the result is not tailored to us):** pick one real or realistic gig: band name, venue, date, time, ticket link, one photo, two band colours, one old flyer for style. Use a free account, then the cheapest paid plan only if free blocks a step. Don't use anything you know about our product.

**Do, and write down the time for each step:**
1. Make a gig flyer from the gig details. Note: how many steps, did you start from a template or from text?
2. Turn it into an Instagram story (9:16) and a square post. Note: automatic or manual?
3. Write captions for the announcement and a reminder. Is there AI help? Does it sound like the band?
4. Save the band's identity (colours, fonts, logo). Then make a second gig campaign. Note: did the tool remember the identity without re-doing it?
5. Plan the posting schedule. Can the tool do it, or do you leave?
6. Try the AI features. Does the output look generic or like the band? Show two outputs.

**Record:** total time for the full set (flyer, story, post, two captions); what plan and price you needed; the three things that annoyed you most; whether you would stop here and not look for another tool.

**How to send it:** reply in this thread with the notes (rough is fine; screenshots help). I'll record them as first-hand evidence from you, marked as one person's trial, not a sample.
