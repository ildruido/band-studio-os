# R-004 addendum 1: widening the scan (round 2)

Follows the skeptic review in [report.md](report.md). Accessed 2026-11-04. Product prices are the vendor's own, grade D, from pages opened today. Anything not opened is a lead with no weight.

## Result in one paragraph

Round 2 did not reach saturation: each new search still found new AI poster and social tools, so the **AI poster and caption class is large and growing, not a short list**. That is itself a finding: nobody has a gig-specific product, but a band can already reach several cheap general or poster-focused AI tools. S1 stays Contradicted. For S2, three tools now sit close to the three conditions, so S2 cannot be called supported and needs the hands-on check ([hands-on-check.md](hands-on-check.md)).

## What this means for the product (one line)

The defensible gap is not "AI makes a flyer" (many cheap tools do), it is the guided gig campaign tied to a band's own identity and an approval flow. R-005 should score that, not poster generation.
