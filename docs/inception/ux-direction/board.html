What changes from version 1

Version 1Direction for version 2
Forms, sliders, tabs and panelsOne decision per screen: pick between two, tap a blank in a sentence, swipe a poster.
Generic dark app with a purple-blue glowConcrete wall, black ink, yellow tape, photocopy grain. No gradients, no glass.
The app has its own colourThe app has none beyond tape yellow. The band's art carries all colour, so your poster is the star.
Bandmate view is a smaller copy of the driver'sA bandmate sees one poster and two buttons, nothing else.
Export is a file listA proof sheet of the finished posters and a countdown plan.

Three rules behind it

Content first

The poster fills the screen. Controls sit at the thumb, in plain words: Rougher, Cleaner, Another.

The app steps back

A fixed backstage look means a band's style never clashes with the app, and it signals "made by people who go to gigs".

Few choices, each one meaningful

Every tap teaches the identity something. We ask less and let the band react to real output, which is how the blind test will judge us anyway.

Look

Archivo, stretched wide for names and squeezed tall for dates, like gig posters. Courier Prime for tour-diary detail. Real posters in this style are photocopied, so grain and slight misregistration are on purpose.

Not shown or not tested: real phone use, animation and swipe gestures (static here), Italian copy, and whether real generated art can reach this level. The posters here are drawn by hand in code.
