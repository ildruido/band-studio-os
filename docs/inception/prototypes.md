## Prototype v1: what it is

- **Sample band:** Cenere Rossa (post-punk, Bologna), playing Sottoscala 13 on 12 December 2026, with Lupi di Periferia. All invented.
- **Screens:** welcome, identity (genre, Grit slider with live preview, anti-moodboard, colours, sample reference images), gig, a short "making your pack" wait, pack (flyer with 3 variants, 3 stories, feed post, IT/EN captions you can edit), band approval, the bandmate's phone view (approve, or veto with a note), export (file list and posting schedule).
- **What is real:** Grit, colours, band name and gig details change all the artwork at once, so the "one identity, many coherent assets" idea can be seen. Regenerate cycles three canned looks. A veto needs a note and allows one revision. Export stays locked until everything is approved.
- **What is faked:** no AI, no sign-in, no upload, no real link, no download, no video. The artwork is drawn from type, colour and texture only, so it shows the idea and is **not** evidence of output quality. That stays with the E2 blind test.
- **Language:** English interface, Italian sample content and Italian captions (with an English switch), see D-030.
- **Direct links** (add to the address): `#identity`, `#gig`, `#pack`, `#approval`, `#review` (bandmate view), `#export`.
- **Interview shortcut:** "Approve everything" on the approval screen skips the bandmate step.
- **Privacy:** the link is private to Ale. For interviews, share it from the artifact's Share menu.

## Suggested use in a driver interview

Show the identity and pack screens only after the driver describes how they promote gigs today. Ask them to react out loud: "is this us?", "what would you change first?", "who in the band would veto this?". Do not present it as the finished product.

## Prototype v2.1: later journeys

- **What it is:** [prototype-v2-1.html](ux-direction/prototype-v2-1.html). The v2 flow is untouched; later screens are added and marked. v2 stays as the confirmed baseline.
- **How to tell MVP from later:** later screens have a hazard-tape stripe on top and a black **Later** tag (or **Idea** for gig night). The "interviewer's map" (link on the welcome screen, or `#map`) lists every screen as First version, Later or Idea.
- **Ways in:** the welcome screen link, "Later: watch the wall grow" on the home screen, and "What comes after this pack" at the bottom of the finished pack.
- **Direct links:** `#map`, `#wall-w1`, `#wall-m1`, `#wall-m6`, `#wall-y1`, `#newsheet`, `#tonight`, `#after`, `#release`, `#tour`, `#reel`, `#channels`, `#us`, `#pay`, `#archive`, plus the v2 ones.
- **Faked:** no video is made, no account is linked, nobody is charged, prices show "€ —", dates are illustrations, posters are drawn in code.
- **Left out on purpose:** chat mode, reading an Instagram or Bandcamp link (parked, D-034), several bands, photos in the thank-you.
- **Not tested:** real phone, real swipes, dark theme, Italian copy, an interview.

## Prototype v2.2: ready for test sessions

- **What it is:** [prototype-v2-2.html](ux-direction/prototype-v2-2.html). v2 and v2.1 stay as they are. v2.2 is the one to put in front of a real band.
- **The story a visitor sees:** a home page that pitches the product, a three-step setup that asks the band's basics (name and city, who is in the band, where they promote and in which language), then the Identity Studio.
- **Identity Studio:** seven chapters, one question per screen, any order, any time: the sound (genre, energy, words for the sound), influences (who people compare you to, where you play), vibe (three words, "if your band were a place"), tone of voice (and a tagline picked from lines matched to that tone), the look (five either-or picks), never (things and words), and lore (two small stories). The answers change the posters and captions: the band's own name, hashtags, vibe words and tagline appear on them. A card on the Us screen reads the answers back as a short description of the band.
- **Navigation:** three tabs on the main screens (Wall, Make, Us). Every other screen has Back and Home, a small heading above the title, and one line saying what the page is for. The first-version pack flow shows "1 of 4" to "4 of 4".
- **Session menu (for the facilitator):** the strip at the top of every screen opens a list of every journey, grouped as story, first version and later. It also has direct links to each Identity Studio chapter, "Load the sample band", "Start over" and "Hide this bar" (leaves a thin stripe; tap it to bring the menu back).
- **Real band or sample band:** a real name typed in setup flows onto every poster. "Use the sample band" fills Cenere Rossa instead. Empty gig fields show greyed sample values.
- **Fixed since v2.1:** thumbnails in the approval list now sit in fixed boxes so a name can never run under a poster; a popup could stay open after "Change words" (found while testing v2.2, and present in v2.1 too).
- **Direct links:** `#pitch`, `#onb1`, `#studio`, `#home`, `#gig`, `#pack`, `#sent`, `#review`, `#ready`, `#wall`, `#tonight`, `#after`, `#release`, `#tour`, `#reel`, `#channels`, `#pay`, `#archive`.
- **Faked:** no AI, no sign-in, no upload, no real link, no download, no video. The taglines are canned lines per tone, the captions are filled from templates, and nothing is saved when the page closes.
- **Tested:** every journey clicked through in a headless browser at 360, 375, 380, 390, 430 and 450 pixels wide, with automatic checks for text running off the screen and for pictures overlapping text. No such problem was found. No script errors.
- **Not tested:** a real phone, the Claude in-app browser itself (the thumbnail overlap seen in v2.1 could not be reproduced in the headless browser, so the fix is by construction, not confirmed), real swipe gestures, dark theme, Italian copy read by an Italian speaker, and an interview.
