# Product Brief Pack

The single place that holds everything a product brief and stakeholder material need for Band Studio OS (working title), in plain language. Decks, a one-pager, a video pitch and briefs for engineers or sales are all built **from this folder**, by Claude here or by external tools. If a fact is not in the pack, it is not ready to be used.

**This is a concept-and-plan pack, not a proof pack.** Field research (interviews, a blind output test, a price test) is postponed, so most claims about real bands are untested. The pack says so on every claim and keeps a separate [gap record](gaps.md) with a red, amber or green rating per component.

Decision behind it: see the [decision log](../inception/decision-log.md). The inventory it was built from: [product-brief-pack.md](../inception/product-brief-pack.md).

## How to read the status tags

Every fact in the pack carries one of these tags, so a deck never presents a guess as a fact.

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Decided** | Ale decided it. It is a choice, not a claim about the world. |
| **Provisional** | Written down and reasonable, but it rests on guesses or on desk research only. |
| **Hypothesis** | A claim we believe but have not tested with real people. |
| **Needs primary research** | Desk research ran and could not settle it. Only talking to or testing with real bands can. |
| **Illustrative** | An example made up to explain something. Never present it as real. |
| **Not yet known** | Nothing usable exists. The section says what is missing. |

## Rules for writing in this pack

- Plain language. No bare codes: say what a decision or a study says and link to it.
- Never invent a fact. A missing fact stays visible as a gap.
- Every number carries its range, its date and how sure we are.
- Authenticity first and human in the loop apply to the pack too: nothing here is published without Ale.
