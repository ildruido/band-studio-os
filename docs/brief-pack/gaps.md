# Gap record

One entry per component of the [Product Brief Pack](index.md): how complete the writing is, how well it is backed by evidence, what is missing, and how it would be filled. It is kept apart from the content on purpose, so the pack reads cleanly and the caveats cannot get lost. Re-rate it after every test.

## How to read the ratings

Every component has **two** ratings, because "we can write it" and "it is proven" are different things.

| Rating | **Content**: can we write it today from what exists? | **Evidence**: is it backed by real-world proof? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟢 Green | Written, complete enough to use, in plain words | Backed by independent, tested evidence |
| 🟡 Amber | Partly written, or written but needs Ale's check or a missing piece | Desk research or a decision only; not tested with real bands |
| 🔴 Red | Little or nothing usable | No evidence, untested core claim, or contradicted |
| ⚪ n/a | | The component is a decision (scope, constraints), not a claim about the world |

## Overall reading

- **Content:** 10 green, 15 amber, 8 red (of 33). Green means the text can be used as is; amber needs Ale's check or a missing piece; red means little or nothing exists yet.
- **Evidence:** 0 green, 6 amber, 15 red, 12 not applicable (decisions). **No component is green on evidence.** Nothing has been tested with real bands.
- **So this is a concept-and-plan pack, not a proof pack.** Any deck or video built from it must say "not yet tested" wherever the evidence rating is red or amber.
- **What moves the most ratings:** the driver interviews, the blind output test and the price test (all postponed), then a pilot. After that, a few decisions from Ale (name, investor ask, founder story).
