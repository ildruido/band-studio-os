# Evidence and risks

Part of the [Product Brief Pack](index.md). This file is the honest centre of the pack: any deck built from the pack should carry its message.

## Traction and validation

**In plain words:** **Nothing has been tested with real bands yet.** There are no interviews, no pilot, no waitlist and no test results. What exists is desk research (reading what is published) and three designed experiments waiting for Ale to say go. Do not present any of this as traction.

**What has run (desk research only)**
- A reality check on whether the target group exists and has the problem: *needs primary research*; consistent with the idea, nothing contradicts it, nothing strong supports it.
- A size check on how many bands: *needs primary research*; very wide range.
- A competitor scan and a competitive analysis: verdict *reshape*, accepted by Ale; position moved to the creative and identity layer.
- A check of the gap against Canva and similar: *needs primary research*.
- A market sizing: *needs primary research*.

**What is designed but not run (postponed until Ale says go)**

| Test | What it would show | Pass mark | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Driver interviews** (8 to 10 Italian band drivers) | Whether drivers exist, rank promotion among their top five problems unprompted, what they use today, what they pay. Also recruits pilot bands | At least 6 of 8 do the promotion themselves and 5 of 8 name it unprompted | Designed, parked |
| **Blind "is this us?" output test** | Whether our output feels like the band, against their own flyer and against Canva or Simplified output. First AI cost data | At least 60% rate it 4 or 5 out of 5, and it ties or beats their flyer in half the pairs | Plan only |
| **Fake-door price test** | Whether bands would commit money | At least 3 bands leave a deposit or written commitment, at least 5% of qualified visitors | Plan only |
| **Pilot** with 5 to 10 bands | Activation, "feels like us", time saved, repeat use | See the roadmap's second decision point | After a build; not started |

**What the free pilot cannot show:** willingness to pay. The price test carries that.

**Not run, still wanted:** a desk study on music and image rights; a short size check by email; a short-video proof of concept.

**How sure are we:** *Not yet known.* The honest line for any outside reader today is "not yet tested".

## Risks and mitigations

Likelihood and impact are **our judgement**, not measured. "Open" means no mitigation yet.

| Risk | Why it matters | Likelihood / impact | What reduces it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Output does not feel like the band** | It is the core promise. Our planned edge is not shown yet and, on today's scoring, we do not beat Canva | Medium to high / very high | Blind output test before building; identity inputs that bands control; human approval before anything leaves |
| **Bands will not pay, or cannot** | Money is the top barrier named by independent artists; most do music part time; many who pay for promotion lose money | High / very high | Price test; pay-per-campaign before subscription; free pilot to learn first; keep AI cost under about 30% of price |
| **Canva, Simplified or others close the gap** | The generic "brief in, campaign out" flow already exists for small businesses; band templates are an easy release | High / high | Position on the band's identity layer, not the generic flow; watch for band pages and pricing moves |
| **Authenticity backlash against AI-made posters** | Some venues (so far US and Canada) refuse AI-made posters | Medium / high | Output clearly the band's own; band curates; nothing published without approval; talk about "your band's material", not "AI posters" |
| **AI generation cost is higher than the price** | Cash limit is under 100 euros a month; video would cost more | Medium / high | Log cost per generation from day one; images only in the first version; price test |
| **Music, likeness and image rights** | Uploaded media and generated assets raise copyright and image-rights questions; a lawyer is outside the budget | Medium / high | No uploads of gig photos or audio in the first version; desk study still to run; reference-image rule |
| **The market is small or shrinking** | UK grassroots shows fell 8.3% since 2023; Italian clubs are in difficulty; the pool of payers is small | Medium / high | Wide-range sizing is honest about it; no upfront size target; income ladder shows how few payers are needed |
| **The problem is real but not top of mind** | Promotion ranked below money and time in the survey we found | Medium / high | Driver interviews, unprompted ranking |
| **Ale's time** | 5 to 10 hours a week; interviews and tests are the long pole | High / medium | No deadline; one decision at a time; gates only on evidence |
| **Weak evidence behind the claims** | Most sources are vendor surveys or press; some forum quotes accepted by Ale could not be opened; Canva pages could not be opened | Certain / medium | Every claim is tagged; research follows a no-bias protocol with an independent skeptic |
| **A reach product owns "promotion"** | Bandsintown Boost sells reach to the same bands | Medium / medium | Treat as a possible partner; stay on the creative layer |
| **Uploading large media files (later)** | Heavy files, cost and rights risk for after-the-gig material | Medium / medium | Out of the first version; edge triage is an architecture question |

**How sure are we:** the *list* is *Provisional* (it comes from desk research and Ale's constraints); the ratings are judgement.

## Proof points

**In plain words:** **There are none.** A proof point is a measured number a customer cares about, such as hours saved or euros saved. Nothing has been measured, so nothing may be claimed.

**What we would measure, and when**
- Time from starting a campaign to export: target 60 minutes of active time or less, compared with the hours bands report today. Measured in the pilot.
- Share of generated assets approved first time with no redo ("feels like us"): target at least 50%. Measured in the blind test, then the pilot.
- Share of new bands that export a first campaign within a week: target at least 50%.
- Share of bands with two or more approved campaigns in 60 days: target at least 40%.
- AI cost per approved campaign: under 30% of price.

All targets are guesses until the first tests.

**One context statistic, not a proof point:** in a survey of musicians in five other countries, 23% spent over half their working time on non-music tasks such as social media. It shows the problem exists elsewhere; it is not about our users and not about our product.

**How sure are we:** *Not yet known.*
