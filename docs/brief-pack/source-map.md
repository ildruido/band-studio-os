# Source map

For each section of the [Product Brief Pack](index.md): which inception documents feed it, what has to be done to the material on the way in, and what no source covers. The last column feeds the [gap record](gaps.md).

Reading tip: sources are listed in order of weight. Documents in `research/` are studies, each with its own verdict and limits; the pack always carries the limit with the fact.
