# Business and go-to-market

> How it would earn, how it would reach bands, and what money it needs.

Part of the [Product Brief Pack](index.md). **Almost everything here is a direction or a guess.** No price has been tested and no channel tried. The sections say so plainly.

## Business model and pricing

**In plain words:** The direction is **pay per campaign** for early users, then a **monthly subscription** for bands that keep coming back, with a free tier and add-ons possible later. The first pilot is free. The actual prices are not set: a price test is designed to find them.

**Key facts**
- *Decided:* pay per campaign first, then subscription; freemium and add-ons later. The first pilot bands pay nothing.
- *Hypothesis:* a band would pay per campaign at a price that also covers the AI cost. The free pilot does **not** test this, so a separate price test ("fake door": a real-looking offer with a deposit or written commitment) carries it. Pass mark: at least three bands commit, and at least 5% of qualified visitors take the offer.
- *Guardrail (provisional):* AI generation cost per approved campaign should be at most 30% of the price. Total cash spend must stay under 100 euros a month until the first paying customers.
- *Price anchors seen in the market, not our prices:* Canva Pro about 13 to 18 USD a month; Simplified Pro about 19 USD a month plus usage; Krumzi about 49 USD for a one-month pass; Bandsintown Boost about 50 USD per show (reach, not design). The market sizing assumes 60 to 300 euros a year per paying band (2 to 8 campaigns at 10 to 40 euros, plus an optional subscription), base 150 euros. This is an assumption, not research.
- *Not known:* the price points, the real cost per campaign, the margin.

**How sure are we:** the direction is *Decided*; every number is a *Hypothesis*. Money is also the biggest barrier named by independent artists in the largest survey we opened, which makes willingness to pay the second riskiest assumption (after "output feels like us").

## Go-to-market

**In plain words:** Start in **Italy**, then the **UK**, then the rest of Western, Northern and Eastern Europe. The first users are 5 to 10 Italian bands who join a free pilot, recruited through the driver interviews. How we reach and win them beyond that is not worked out.

**Key facts**
- *Decided:* launch market order (Italy, then UK, then wider Europe). Italian-language and EU-law first.
- *Provisional:* the pilot is 5 to 10 Italian bands, each running at least one real gig campaign, found through the interviews.
- *Not yet known:* which channels reach bands (scenes, venues, social groups), what they cost, and who talks to bands. The interview plan names recruiting routes but none has been used. Paid acquisition at scale is a non-goal under the cash limit; only small tests fit.
- *Later:* UK launch and English after Italy has paying bands.

**How sure are we:** the market order is *Decided*; everything about reaching bands is *Not yet known*.

## Financials and the ask

**In plain words:** There is **no investor ask and no financial plan**. What exists is a spending limit and some arithmetic about what revenue would need.

**Key facts**
- *Decided:* under 100 euros a month in total (tools, hosting, AI generation, test ads) until the first paying customers. Ale works part time (about 5 to 10 hours a week) with Claude; no hires.
- *Arithmetic, not a forecast:* at 150 euros a year per paying band, 30 thousand euros a year needs about 200 paying bands and 100 thousand needs about 670.
- *Targets (guesses):* 20 approved campaigns a month within three months of the first paid pilot and 100 a month within a year.
- *Not decided:* whether to raise money at all, how much, and for what. This needs Ale's decision before any investor material is useful.

**How sure are we:** the spending limit is *Decided*; all else is *Not yet known*.

## Offer and pricing sheet

**In plain words:** Today's offer is the **free pilot**: a pilot band gets the first version at no cost, in return for using it on at least one real gig and giving feedback, and is asked at the end whether they would commit to paying for a campaign. There is no paid offer yet.

**Key facts**
- *Decided:* free for the first pilot bands.
- *Not yet known:* paid offer, price per campaign, subscription tiers, what is free and what is not.

**How sure are we:** the pilot offer is *Decided*; the paid offer is *Not yet known*.

## Launch and channel plan

**In plain words:** There is **no launch plan yet**. We know where we start (Italy) and how the first bands are found (interviews, then a pilot). Where bands can be reached, and what to say in each place, is still to work out.

**Key facts**
- *Starting point:* Italy first; pilot bands from the interviews.
- *Not yet known:* channels (venues, local scenes, social groups, music schools, festivals), messages per channel, timing, cost. Forum and group channels were hard to research from here: Reddit and Facebook groups could not be opened.

**How sure are we:** *Not yet known.*
